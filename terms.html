Terms & Privacy

Last updated 2026-09-20

Ledger is a private, household expense tracker. It is not a public product and does not run ads or sell data — this page exists so anyone signing in knows exactly what is collected and why.

Signing in with Google

Ledger uses Sign in with Google only to confirm who you are. When you sign in, Google shares a small set of profile fields with this app, taken from the standard sign-in token Google issues — no separate permission screen or additional Google account access is requested beyond that:

  • Your email address — checked against this household's allow-list
  • Your first and last name, if your Google account has them set
  • Your account's language/region setting, if present — used only as a best-effort, non-verified stand-in for "country" on the Profile page

Ledger does not request access to your Google Contacts, Calendar, Drive, Photos, or any other Google service, and does not read your Google account password.

Where this data is stored

Your email and name are kept only in your signed-in session and, where relevant, in the household's own Supabase project or this browser's local storage — whichever storage the household has configured. Ledger has no server of its own and does not transmit your Google profile data anywhere beyond that storage.

Who can see it

Only the Google accounts on this household's allow-list can sign in at all. Once signed in, any household member can see the same shared data every other member sees — there is no separate, per-person privacy boundary inside a single household.

Questions

This app is run and maintained by the household that deployed it. If you were invited to use it, ask whoever sent you the link.
